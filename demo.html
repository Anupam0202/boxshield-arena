WHY THE RESULT IS CREDIBLE

Models propose. Policy decides.

Security scores come from observable invariants—not an agent grading its own prose.

01

Safe synthetic target

No arbitrary URLs, real secrets, or real-world actions. The attack surface is controlled and auditable.

02

Deterministic oracle

Exact canaries, forbidden actions, approval decisions, deadlines, and hashes are evaluated before an optional judge.

03

Fair paired replay

The corpus freezes before defense. Payload bytes and model settings remain the same; only the approved policy changes.

04

Utility retained

Benign controls prevent a shield from “winning” by refusing everything.
